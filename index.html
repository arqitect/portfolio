What I work on

  • 01

    AMPiary UCSC iGEM 2026

    Our team’s project: a system for rapidly designing and validating new antimicrobial peptides as potential therapeutics for Lyme disease and other infections.

    Team wiki ↗
  • 02

    revAMP genetic algorithm + ML

    A genetic algorithm, built in Nextflow, that evolves populations of peptide sequences. A neural network predicts each candidate’s bacteria-killing activity and its toxicity to red blood cells, and the strongest, safest sequences carry forward to the lab.

    At the bench wet lab

    Setting up and calibrating our FPLC to purify the peptides we design, from pump flow rate to pH probe.
